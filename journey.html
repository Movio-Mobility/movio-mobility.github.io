2018 Where it started

Combat robotics geeks

Hriteek and Shivanshu met in the official robotics club at VIT Vellore and clicked instantly. A mechanical engineer and an electronics engineer, they spent four years building combat robots together, and learned to take on hard problems as a pair.

  • Shivanshu MahimCold-texts legends. Some of them text back.
Continue to 2021 Electric skateboards

2021 From campus rides to a company

Electric skateboards

VIT is a huge campus, so Hriteek and Shivanshu solved their own commute: they built electric skateboards and scooters to get around it. That is when they fell in love with electric vehicles.

Movio and micro-mobility

Zipping around campus on those boards caught the eye of VIT-TBI, the university's incubator, which helped turn the project into a business. They registered Movio Technologies Private Limited, raised ₹2.2 lakh, and began building personal light electric vehicles in Bangalore.

Talking to riders taught them the real problem was never the vehicle. It was the battery.

Continue to 2022 The coder arrives

2022 Software, and a grant

The coder arrives

The team needed serious software depth. Shivanshu called an old friend from Kota, Vishrut Vatsa, then at Boeing R&D, who had built software for IISc Bangalore and interned at Apple. He was invited in as a co-founder, and left Boeing to start from zero with Movio.

  • Vishrut VatsaTaught a battery to talk. It hasn't stopped since.

Government grant

Full-stack prototypes, a made-in-India approach and careful UI/UX caught the attention of the incubators at IIT Roorkee and VIT-TBI. Through the NIDHI PRAYAS scheme and MeitY's TIDE 2.0 scale-up scheme, Movio secured ₹40 lakh in all.

That was the turning point. The founders went full-time on the EV battery problem.

Continue to 2023 The team builder arrives

2023 Delhi, a team, a product

The team builder arrives

The team moved to Delhi for its supportive EV policy and its ecosystem for hardware companies, and completed its founding team with Paridhi. She and Shivanshu are cousins who grew up together, and her background in economics and human resource management balanced a team of engineers.

Building a team in a new city was the first priority. It would have been far harder without her.

  • Paridhi SinhaKeeps the money sensible and the engineers fed.

Team building

From a small office in Gurgaon, the founders built a lean team of engineers and business developers. The tech team prototyped hardware while the business team sat with customers to find what hurt. Build, learn, iterate, repeat.

Prototyping

Four battery pack prototypes and two swap hub iterations later, they had a minimum viable product. They tested the whole stack with a small group of riders to learn what worked and what didn't.

MVP

The MVP went to high-intent riders who are on their vehicles all day, from Swiggy delivery executives to heavy-cargo logistics. It also proved something important: riders love charging at home, as long as they can swap when they need to.

GridXenergy

The MVP and conversations with industry experts called for real change. The market was moving to high-speed EVs and personal mobility, so Movio became GridXenergy. The packs grew more powerful and more carefully detailed, and their design and portability were rethought for personal riders.

Continue to 2024 Product showcasing

2024 Out in the world

Product showcasing

With the industrial design prototypes complete, GridX went on show to all kinds of audiences, from the Uttarakhand Investor Summit to college campuses in Pune.

SUN Mobility

Shivanshu cold-texted Chetan Maini of SUN Mobility, and the team went to meet the godfather of India's EV industry. They toured SUN's manufacturing plant, pitched their consumer brand and showed their technology to seasoned engineers.

They also found a mentor in Anand Ganapathy Chennira, SUN's Chief Strategy Officer, who has guided them since December 2023.

Early traction

GridX demonstrated its full stack to the EV maker GT Force and secured its first Letter of Intent. A respected fleet owner in Delhi NCR followed with a pre-order for 100 vehicles, on the strength of the working prototypes alone.

Rigorous testing

A dedicated rider put the PowerPod and the app through daily operations, long routes and heavy use. The data and feedback from those rides sharpened the system's stability and the app, and proved the technology ready for the road.

Continue to 2025 First customer

2025 The first customer

First customer

At a local dealership, the team met a rider tired of battery failures and costly replacements. She was skeptical of battery-as-a-service at first.

Nahi nahi, humein wahi wali battery chahiye. No, no, we want the same old battery.

The team walked her through it: nothing upfront, no maintenance, pay per use. Skepticism turned to curiosity, then trust, and she retrofitted her vehicle. It wasn't just a sale. It was proof that GridX solves a real problem.

Out of the garage

For a year, GridX lived where it was built: a garage where we worked, cooked and slept. Then we moved into our own office and R&D floor in Okhla, and our first interns walked straight into the lab and the field.

400 kWh on the road

While the new office came together, PowerPods were already out on the road. Early riders drew more than 400 kWh of clean energy from them, and stayed happy doing it.

Gen2 begins

We started on Gen2: a PowerPod built for AIS certification and for scale. It brought our own 100 A symmetric connector and cell-level destructive testing.

Vecmocon, which already powers Bajaj, joined us on the BMS and chargers.

Software, rebuilt

We rebuilt the backend from its core: faster, sturdier, ready to scale. At its heart sits Paddock, where our systems talk to each other and new ideas get tried without a rider ever noticing.

Invisible work that powers the visible magic.

Continue to 2026 Built to the new rules

2026 From prototype to the road

Built to the new rules

India's battery safety rules changed. AIS 156 Amendment 4 asked for a 1.5 mm gap between every cell, so we redesigned Gen2 on the inside without changing a thing on the outside.

We built our own fuse, heat-tested the pack for every Indian season, and locked it for production.

Patented, and backed

We filed a patent for the PowerPod's symmetric design. And Chitkara University chose to back us: aligned capital to bring production closer.

Our own line

We built our first 50 PowerPods ourselves, on our own line. Slower on paper, faster in practice: every tolerance, failure point and fix is now ours, written down before we scale.

More of us

The team grew across mechanical and software, and our first intern became a full-timer. Everyone here chose to be here.

A bike joins the platform

One Electric's bike now runs on the GridX platform, PowerPod docked. A different vehicle, a different power system, a different rider. The same energy.

On the big stages

We finished second at Battery 360, in a room full of serious battery builders. We showed PowerPod at TiEcon Chandigarh with Chitkara, then took it national at Battery Summit on 16 June.

The market found us

One reel travelled further than we planned: 50K+ views, 700+ riders asking where to buy, and 20+ dealerships asking to sell.

So we stopped waiting. Our first dealerships brought PowerPod into their stores. Find one near you.

Own it, or just use it

Riders wanted PowerPod for different reasons. Some just want the energy, through Energy-as-a-Service. Others want to own the battery outright.

Same PowerPod, two ways in.

Built beyond our walls

Stefen Electric, with room for 200 PowerPods a day, built the first one outside GridX, on a line set up to our process. It is already out with a rider.

We filed a process patent, and Gen3 is under way. More on that soon.

Today

Who we are

GridX is an energy company. We started in 2021 as Movio Technologies Private Limited, building electric rides on a college campus, and learned that the part worth reinventing was the energy itself.

So that is what we design, all of it, under one roof: packs shaped for very different lives, the electronics that look after every cell, and the software that reaches from inside the pack to the phone in your hand. Each part is made knowing the others, which is why they behave as one.

PowerPod was the first of them, built for India's two-wheelers, where stored energy works harder every day than almost anywhere else. It is where we began, not where we stop. Our mission is to make stored energy so dependable, portable and affordable that running out is no longer something anyone has to plan around.
