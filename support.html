  • Every PowerPod is manufactured with the utmost attention to detail, and with safety built in at every level: ultra-safe NMC cells in a rugged aluminium body, a battery management system watching over them, and protection against over voltage, under voltage and short circuits. A fire is highly unlikely. But in that rare event:

    1. Move away to a safe distance. Do not touch it or move it.
    2. Call the fire service and tell them: "A lithium-ion battery is on fire."
    3. Do not try to put the fire out yourself. Leave it to the fire service.
    4. Stay out of the smoke, which can be toxic. If you are indoors, get out, and only open windows if it is safe to.
    5. Do not go back to it afterwards. A lithium-ion battery can reignite hours later, so wait for the fire service to say it is safe.
