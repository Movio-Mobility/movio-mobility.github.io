Version 1.0. For PowerPod products bought in India through an Authorised PowerPod Dealer.

This Limited Product Warranty and Guarantee is provided by Movio Technologies Private Limited, operating under the brand name GridXenergy ("GridX", "we", "us" or "our"). It covers the PowerPod Battery, the PowerPod Fast Charger and the PowerPod Vehicle Dock, on the terms, conditions, exclusions and limitations set out here.

The first four sections say what the warranty means in plain words. The warranty in full follows them, and where the two read differently, the full terms apply.

To raise a claim, start with your Authorised Dealer (find one near you), or reach us through Support.

In plain words

I.What is covered: the promise

This is what you get with the 3-year standard warranty, and with the 5-year cover (3 + 2) if you add the extension when you buy.

  • Manufacturing defects. We cover any failure caused by how the battery was built. If the internal spot welding, the thermal management or the Battery Management System (BMS) fails under normal use, it is on us.
  • Performance degradation. Batteries naturally lose a little capacity over time. If your PowerPod's maximum capacity drops below 70% of its original rating during the warranty period, we repair or replace it.
  • Connector integrity. The hot-swappable connector is covered when it fails through normal mating cycles, not through forced physical damage.
  • Labour and parts. Internal cell replacements, the electronics and the labour to fix them are fully covered. You pay nothing out of pocket for a valid claim.

II.What to expect: the experience

How it goes when something goes wrong.

  • Data-driven diagnostics. The PowerPod's BMS keeps telemetry logs, so we can diagnose an issue from the data without making you jump through hoops.
  • Quick resolution. A faulty pod gets a streamlined repair or a direct swap for a working pod, to get you back on the road.
  • Hassle-free transfers. If you sell your vehicle or hand over your energy subscription, the warranty goes with the pod to the new user.
  • Firmware updates. Over-the-air and service-centre updates keep improving the battery's software, for efficiency and for safety.

III.What is not covered: the exclusions

Damage from outside the pack, and what the PowerPod was never meant to go through.

  • Physical abuse and accidents. Damage from dropping the PowerPod, vehicle crashes or severe external impact.
  • Tampering and unauthorised access. Opening the casing, breaking the tamper seals or attempting to modify the internal circuit boards or cell configuration ends the warranty.
  • Improper charging. Using non-approved, third-party chargers, or modifying the charging cables.
  • Extreme neglect. Leaving the battery completely drained, at 0%, for a long period (over 30 days, for example), which chemically damages lithium-ion cells.
  • Environmental damage. Submersion in water beyond the pod's IP rating, or exposure to external fires or natural disasters.

IV.Our responsibility: the manufacturer's onus

We design, engineer and manufacture these packs from the ground up, so these are our commitments.

  • Design integrity. We own the safety of the pack. If a thermal event or a failure is caused by a flaw in our layout or our choice of materials, we take full responsibility for replacing the hardware.
  • Accurate telemetry. We are responsible for the BMS reporting state of charge (SoC) and state of health (SoH) accurately. If the software gives you fundamentally false readings, we must correct it.
  • Supply chain quality. If a batch of lithium-ion cells from our supplier is faulty, it is on us to replace your pod. The dispute with the supplier is ours to handle.

6.Battery capacity warranty

Battery capacity naturally changes over time and with use.

During the applicable Battery Warranty Period, where the maximum measured usable capacity of the PowerPod™ Battery falls below 70% of its original rated capacity, GridX will assess the Battery through its applicable diagnostic and testing process.

Where GridX confirms that the applicable capacity threshold has been reached and the condition is covered under this Warranty, GridX shall, at its discretion, repair or replace the affected Battery or relevant components.

The capacity assessment shall be conducted using GridX's applicable testing methodology, diagnostic equipment, BMS data and/or other technical information considered appropriate by GridX.

The capacity threshold shall not be determined solely on the basis of the customer's observed vehicle range or distance travelled per charge.

Battery capacity and performance may be affected by usage, charging patterns, storage conditions, operating temperature, vehicle configuration, load, riding conditions and other factors. Accordingly, the 70% capacity provision is not a guarantee of vehicle range or kilometres per charge.

Failure of the connector attributable to normal intended mating and de-mating cycles may be covered under the applicable Battery Warranty.

Connector damage resulting from forced insertion or removal, impact, bending, crushing, contamination, misuse, improper installation, unauthorised modification or other physical damage is not covered.

GridX shall determine, based on inspection and/or diagnostic assessment, whether a connector issue is attributable to Manufacturing Defect, intended usage, normal wear and tear or an excluded circumstance.
